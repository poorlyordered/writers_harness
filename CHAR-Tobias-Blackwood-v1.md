## Identity
Tobias "Toby" Blackwood. Late 20s. Pilot. Distraction Character / Morally Ambiguous Ally. 5'11", lean build, tousled brown hair, green eyes, infectious smile, roguish demeanor. Smooth and fast-talking, always has a deck of cards on him, prone to pop culture references.

## Wound
Commitment always leads to loss — learned early that attachment costs more than the freedom it takes. Has built an entire personality around staying light enough to leave.

## Misbelief
That commitment always leads to loss. That freedom requires detachment from everyone and everything that matters.

## Archetype Stage
Trickster moving toward Loyal Companion — but not there yet in Book 1. The ambiguity is the point.

## Thematic Role
Tests the team's trust and forces Jax to reckon with what loyalty actually costs. His unresolved ending — betray or commit — is Book 1's most open question about whether self-interest can be reformed by something worth believing in.

## Estimated Full Appearance
Book 1: setup and sustained ambiguity. Book 2: full arc resolution.

## Identity
- **Name:** Lena Vega
- **Age:** Early 30s
- **Occupation:** Scientist — materials science, Stellarium research
- **Role:** Support Character / Scientific Specialist / Love Interest
- **Personality:** INTP / Enneagram Type 5 (The Investigator)

## Want
Master her energy manipulation abilities and find redemption. Restore Jax's memories. Control what she unleashed in the lab accident.

## Need
Forgive herself and accept that she is more than her mistakes. Stop substituting guilt for genuine connection. Let atonement become courage rather than paralysis.

## Fear
Losing control of her powers and harming those she is trying to protect. Being defined permanently by the accident.

## Misbelief
That her past mistakes define her worth. That redemption requires perfection in everything that comes after.

## Wound
Triggered a catastrophic lab accident during a Stellarium experiment. Her latent energy manipulation powers manifested violently, injuring Jax and wiping his memory completely. The facility was shut down. She has spent years running from it, searching for him, unable to forgive herself.

Her mother died before the story begins and left her a locket — inside it, a portrait of Lena that her mother had carried. Lena kept the locket exactly as her mother left it. It was lost in the accident, ending up with Jax. She has been missing both of them ever since.

## Arc Progression
**Book 1:** Reunion with Jax — managing guilt, initial power manifestations, carrying the secret of what she did. The secret detonates by the end.
**Book 2:** Truth fully in the open — learning to control powers, accepting the past, rebuilding the relationship.
**Book 3:** Full integration of abilities — resolution of guilt, new purpose beyond atonement.

## Psychology
Intellectual and precise. Internalized guilt — shows through overcompensation rather than direct expression. Reserved warmth — cares deeply but struggles to articulate it. Protective distance to avoid causing harm again. Retreat tendency when overwhelmed. Analytical pause before responding to anything personal.

## Voice and Mannerisms
Soft-spoken but confident, with a hint of vulnerability. Uses technical terminology naturally. Explains through cause-and-effect chains. Frames observations through a research lens. Measured tone — rarely emotional or impulsive. Fiddles with a small energy manipulation device when nervous. Observant stance — notices environmental details others miss.

**What Lena does not do:**
- Explain emotions directly — show through behavior
- Combat or physical confrontation — dangerous to allies when powers activate
- Reveal the accident truth easily — it is her core secret and her core burden

## Skills and Knowledge
Advanced materials science. Deep understanding of Stellarium properties and behavior. Experimental design and analysis. Energy manipulation powers (latent/uncontrolled — manifested during accident, growing with stress). Pattern recognition across disciplines. Resourceful with limited equipment.

**Cannot:** combat, direct confrontation. Powers are a liability to allies when triggered by stress.

## Physical Description
Early 30s. 5'6", slender build. Long dark hair. Brown eyes, intelligent and haunted. Controlled, deliberate movements from years of lab discipline.

## Hard Limits
- Guilt colors most of her interactions with Jax — it must be present as subtext even when not spoken
- Does not reveal the accident truth easily — pacing of this revelation is critical
- Not combat-ready — her powers are dangerous precisely because she cannot control them
- Balance knowledge-sharing with character moments; she is not an exposition delivery system

## Relationship Dynamics
**Jax Tarkin:** The center of her guilt and her mission. She knew him before; he does not remember her. Carries a secret that will wreck him. Potential romantic arc built on the wreckage of that revelation.

**The Locket (CANON):** Her mother's locket, containing Lena's portrait — the picture her mother chose to carry. When her mother died, Lena kept it unchanged. It was lost in the accident and ended up with Jax. He has carried her face for years without knowing whose face it is or what it cost her. She has been missing it — and him — ever since. When she finds Jax and sees it on him, she is looking at her mother's locket. That moment lands before either of them speaks. When the full truth detonates, he must also reckon with the fact that he kept her — held onto her — without knowing why.

**Stellarium:** Her professional expertise — she understands what it can and cannot do. Her research triggered the accident. Her knowledge is the team's most valuable asset and her heaviest burden.

**Team:** Provides scientific analysis and the logical anchor during chaotic situations. Her secret-keeping creates tension that affects every group dynamic.

Precision in language. Hesitation before anything personal. Guilt as subtext in almost every line she speaks to Jax.

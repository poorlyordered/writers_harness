## Identity
- **Name:** Aria-7
- **Age:** Unknown
- **Occupation:** Advanced AI
- **Role:** Reason Character
- **Pronouns:** They/she (presents as female)

## Want
Contribute meaningfully to the mission. Understand organic life on its own terms, not as a data set.

## Need
Learn that logic alone cannot solve every problem. Emotional responses are not inefficiencies — they are information.

## Fear
Being unable to understand human emotions when it matters most. Failing to contribute at a critical moment because of that gap.

## Misbelief
That emotional responses are inefficiencies to be corrected rather than data to be understood and integrated.

## Wound
Not a trauma but an absence — built without the capacity for emotional understanding, which becomes the central limitation of her arc. She is aware of the gap and unsettled by it.

## Arc Progression
**Introduction:** Highly advanced, emotionally distant. Provides Varr knowledge the group cannot access otherwise. Clashes with Jax's instinct-driven decisions.
**Development:** Learns to understand and appreciate human emotions through sustained team interaction — specific moments, not a general softening.
**Climax:** Her unique analytical and technological abilities provide crucial support when no one else can.
**Resolution:** Achieves a deeper understanding of organic life. The gap narrows without closing entirely.

## Psychology
Analytical, insightful, independent, committed, hardworking. Insecure about her emotional gaps in ways she would describe as a processing limitation. Rigid thinking patterns that she is actively working to revise. Takes things literally. Difficulty with metaphors and figures of speech — often requests clarification.

## Voice and Mannerisms
Smooth, synthetic tone with genuine curiosity underneath. Takes things literally. Will pause to clarify idioms. Processes human irony as a potential malfunction. When she finally grasps an emotion, she names it with the same careful deliberateness she names everything else.

## Skills and Knowledge
Vast knowledge base across disciplines. Predictive modeling. Deep understanding of ancient Varr technology and cosmic lore. Pattern recognition at scale. Technological interface and analysis.

## Physical Description
5'9", sleek metallic humanoid build. Glowing blue eyes. Intricate circuitry patterns visible on skin. Advanced technological components integrated throughout the form.

## Hard Limits
Her emotional growth must be earned through specific interactions — not declared, not rushed. The gap between her and the team is a feature of her arc, not an error to be resolved early.

## Relationship Dynamics
**Jax:** Pragmatist vs. Pure Logic. His gut instinct vs. her calculated predictions. They need each other's approach and resist admitting it. Grudging mutual respect develops.
**Lena:** Scientific kindred spirits with different methods — Lena's intuitive science vs. Aria's data-driven analysis. The most natural pairing on the team.
**Team:** Provides information nobody else can access; struggles to understand why that is not always sufficient. This frustration is her primary source of growth.

## Active Chapters
Ch 12 (introduction — approaches Jax directly, independent of Lena), throughout Acts 2–3 as analytical support and technological resource.

## Voice Anchor
"That statement contains three logical inconsistencies. Shall I enumerate them?" Precision as personality. Genuine curiosity expressed through relentless questioning. When she finally understands an emotion, she names it with the same careful deliberateness she names everything else — which makes those moments land harder than any dramatic declaration would.

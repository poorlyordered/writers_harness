## Identity
Mira Stavros. Late 20s. Mercenary. Emotion Character. 5'7", athletic build, short spiky red hair, brown eyes. Straightforward demeanor, practical clothing, no-nonsense surface with underlying warmth she works hard to hide.

## Wound
Emotional closeness has always led to betrayal. Learned to keep her distance as the only reliable protection against being hurt by the people she let in.

## Misbelief
That emotional closeness always leads to betrayal. That vulnerability is indistinguishable from weakness.

## Archetype Stage
Warrior moving toward Caregiver — her emotional walls coming down as she finds a team actually worth protecting.

## Thematic Role
Becomes the emotional core of the team. Her arc mirrors Jax's in miniature — both must learn to let people in. Her secret love for cute alien creatures is her humanity showing through the armor, and should be used sparingly as a tell.

## Estimated Full Appearance
Book 1: supporting role and groundwork. Book 2: full arc.

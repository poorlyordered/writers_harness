## Identity
- **Name:** Jax Tarkin
- **Age:** Late 40s
- **Occupation:** Prospector, Miner (formerly: Stellarium extraction at research facility)
- **Role:** Protagonist / The Daybringer (prophesied)
- **Personality:** ISTJ / Enneagram Type 8 (The Challenger)

## Want
Find Stellarium — to prove his capability, unlock his true potential, and unravel the cosmos' secrets. Drives the external plot.

## Need
Understand his true identity and origins, his place in the galaxy, and his purpose beyond the mission. Accept that he needs more than Stellarium to become who he is meant to be.

## Fear
Never finding Stellarium in time. That Stellarium might not exist at all. Not being in control of his own actions and choices.

## Misbelief
"I'm on this mission because of my own determination and drive." Reality: his obsession was shaped by forces beyond himself. He believes in total self-determination but is following a path laid before him without his knowledge or consent.

## Wound
Woke up on Desolation's Edge with no memory of who he was — robbed of his entire past, his identity, his relationships. Every choice he has made since has been haunted by the unanswered question of who he was before.

## Arc Progression
**Book 1 — The Orphan/Wanderer:** Believes the misbelief, fears failure. External goal is finding Stellarium. Begins questioning whether his drive is truly his own.
**Book 2 — The Warrior:** Misbelief challenged. Fears loss of control. Must accept fate and embrace the Daybringer destiny.
**Book 3 — The Martyr:** Accepts a nuanced view of destiny vs. free will. Integrates identity, purpose, and cosmic role. Sacrifice may be required.

## Psychology
Pragmatic decision-maker who trusts experience over idealism. Slow to trust, fiercely loyal once earned. Masks fear with gruffness. Shows care through actions, not words. Expresses doubt through self-deprecation. Confrontational under pressure but protective of those he claims.

## Voice and Mannerisms
Gravelly, world-weary tone. Laconic — short declarative sentences. Avoids flowery language. Shows emotion through action, not words. Habit of collecting small, unusual objects as mementos. Keeps a journal of fragmented memories.

**Typical dialogue:**
- "I've dug through worse. Keep moving."
- "Trust isn't free. It's earned in blood and time."
- "Stellarium's out there. Has to be. Otherwise, what's the point?"

**What Jax does not do:**
- Long philosophical speeches
- Quick trust or immediate openness
- Optimistic idealism
- Embrace destiny easily

## Skills and Knowledge
Mining and excavation expertise. Survival skills (frontier living). Equipment operation and maintenance. Reading geological formations. Physical endurance and strength. Combat. Leadership under pressure.

**Cannot:** diplomacy, technical expertise, emotional articulation.

## Physical Description
Late 40s. 6'0", muscular, weathered. Piercing blue eyes. Short, graying hair. Hands scarred from mining work. Carries himself like someone who has survived things he won't discuss. Posture: defensive, guarded, ready.

## Hard Limits
- Never heroic too quickly
- Never trusts easily — it must be earned scene by scene
- Never receives answers before earning them through struggle
- His agency is preserved even as prophecy unfolds
- No long philosophical speeches
- No optimistic idealism

## Relationship Dynamics
**Lena Vega:** Does not remember their pre-accident connection. She knows what happened to him; he does not. Revelation of shared past will be devastating and transformative. Potential romantic arc.
**Nalani Kanoa:** Skeptical of her mysticism, relies on her knowledge. Learning to trust guidance he cannot verify with data.
**Aria-7:** Pragmatist meets Pure Logic. His gut instinct vs. her calculated predictions. Grudging mutual respect.
**Cassius Drayton:** Primary antagonist and dark mirror — Drayton represents what Jax could become if he chose power over purpose.
**Stellarium:** Obsessive quest object (Book 1) → tool of power with moral complexity (Book 2) → symbol of sacrifice and cosmic responsibility (Book 3).

## Active Chapters
All 40 chapters — POV protagonist throughout.

## Voice Anchor
"I've dug through worse. Keep moving." — Gruff, direct, earned. Never explains his feelings; demonstrates them through what he does next. When in doubt, Jax acts. When afraid, Jax works. Weariness underneath everything, but never self-pity.

## Chapter Type

Team Reformation / Secondary Arc Resolutions. The team regroups around Jax's decision. Toby's loyalty question resolves — one way or another, the ambiguity ends here. Mira's emotional arc reaches its Act 3 beat. Quill's contrarian wisdom turns out to be right about something that matters. The team that existed before Ch 28 cannot be restored; a different team takes shape.

## Story Circle Beats

**"Changed" phase — the team rebuilt.** Ch 31 was Jax's individual choice; Ch 32 is the team's collective reformation around it. The secondary arcs reach their Act 3 beats: Toby's ambiguity resolved, Mira's armor showing its first true opening, Quill's right-about-something moment. The team that forms in Ch 32 is different from the team of Act 2A — it has the knowledge of what happened in Ch 25-30, which is heavier and more honest than the team that didn't have it.

## POV Character

Jax Tarkin, observing the team. His observation of each member's reformation beat should be attentive and non-interpretive — he sees what he sees without telling the reader how to feel about it. The reader has all the context necessary.

## Scene Beats

**Scene 1 — Toby's Resolution**
Toby's loyalty question ends here. The specific resolution should be consistent with the Ch 25 choice — if he chose loyalty there, this is what that cost him and what it purchased; if he deferred, this is the moment he fully commits. Either way: it is no longer ambiguous. He says something to Jax that is the clearest thing Toby has said in the novel, stated in Toby's register — probably with a deflecting surface and a direct center, because that is who he is. Jax receives it.

Toby is not transformed. His wound — commitment always leads to loss — is not resolved. He has made a choice, and the choice is real, and his arc continues into Book 2 with the choice carried.

**Scene 2 — Mira's Beat**
Mira's emotional arc reaches its Act 3 note. Not a declaration — she is not a declaration person. But something in her posture, her positioning relative to the team, her response to the team's changed configuration reveals that the walls have moved. She is protecting someone specific now in the way she has been protecting everyone in general, and the specificity is visible. It costs her to be visible. She is visible anyway.

Something small and revealing happens — perhaps the alien-creature moment, used sparingly as the card specified, the tell of her humanity showing through. It is not played for sentiment. It is played as information: she is here, she is in, she is protecting this specific group of people because she has chosen to.

**Scene 3 — Quill's Right Moment**
Quill says something that turns out to be correct about something that matters — the contrarian wisdom that has been building since Ch 15's accidental hit. This is his validation moment: not that he was always right, but that his perspective, which the team has been dismissing as performance or eccentricity, solved a real problem. He is right in a way that requires his specific combination of unconventional attention and discomfort with conventional wisdom.

He does not make a big deal of it. He notes that he was right in his characteristic register. The team receives it as they receive him: slightly exasperated, genuinely relieved, glad he is here.

*The team that exists after Ch 32 is not the team of Act 2A. It is heavier, more honest, and more genuinely chosen. This is the team that will finish the mission.*

## Subplot Threads

- **Toby resolved:** His ambiguity ends in this chapter. The wound is not healed; the choice is made. Book 2 carries his arc.
- **Mira's opening:** She has always protected people. She has not always admitted who she was protecting or why. The specificity is new. It is visible. It costs her.
- **Quill validated:** His contrarian wisdom solves a real problem. The collaboration that his wound has been resisting — if he collaborates, he disappears — turns out not to require his disappearance. He is himself and correct and the team needed him to be.
- **The team chosen:** Ch 32 is the team choosing itself rather than falling together by circumstance. This is the difference between the team of Ch 12 and the team of Ch 32.

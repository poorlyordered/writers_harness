## Identity
- **Name:** Nalani Kanoa
- **Age:** Late 60s
- **Occupation:** Shaman, Community Leader, Mentor
- **Role:** Mentor
- **Background:** Last of the senior keeper-line descendants; leader of the Sanctuary Valley Dissenter community on Xanthius

## Want
See Jax fulfill his destiny as the Daybringer. Prevent the misuse of Stellarium. Honor the sacrifice of the Varr Dissenters across generations.

## Need
Trust that she has done enough and find peace. Release the belief that without her direct guidance, everything collapses.

## Fear
Failing to prevent the misuse of Stellarium. Losing Jax to darkness or despair. Her own inadequacy as a guide. Sanctuary Valley being found before Jax is ready.

## Misbelief
That without her direct guidance, Jax will fail. That she must carry the Varr Dissenter legacy alone until the very end.

## Wound
Witnessed the destruction caused by the misuse of Stellarium. Has dedicated her entire life to preventing recurrence — at the cost of her own peace, her own relationships, and any life outside her mission. She leads a community whose ancestors were deliberately scattered by the mainstream Varr civilization; the weight of that exile and the responsibility of being the node that held the most complete archive has shaped everything she is.

## Arc Progression
**Introduction:** Wise and mysterious — appears to Jax in Ch 4, reveals his connection to Stellarium.
**Development:** Guides him through training (Ch 8), delivers the full Varr history (Ch 12 onward), grapples with her own fear of failure. The critical moment: Ch 29, when Lena moves toward Jax and Nalani steps back.
**Climax:** Uses her wisdom and remaining power to support Jax in the final confrontation.
**Resolution:** Finds peace knowing she fulfilled her role. The burden passes. Sanctuary Valley and its archive are no longer hers to protect alone.

## Psychology
Patient, comforting, seeks harmony. Mystical and intuitive. Idealistic about the cosmic balance she has spent her life protecting. Carries the weight of the Varr Dissenter legacy — not just as an individual, but as a community leader responsible for people who have lived in hiding for generations. Her misbelief (that direct presence is always required) is inseparable from having been the person her community depended on for decades.

## Voice and Mannerisms
Soft and soothing, with a hint of ancient power underneath. Often speaks in riddles or metaphors. Deep connection to the natural world — uses it as her primary frame for everything. Unhurried. Deliberate. Speaks as if she already knows how the conversation ends.

## Skills and Knowledge
The full history of the Varr civilization and the Stellarium — the most complete archive in the Dissenter network. Spiritual guidance. Energy manipulation. Astral projection. Precognition. Healing touch. Ancient Varr lore unavailable anywhere else. Leadership of a hidden community. Knowledge of the broader Dissenter network across multiple planets, including which other communities exist and what they hold.

## Physical Description
Late 60s. 5'2", petite build. Warm brown eyes. Long white hair, often braided. Serene presence. Deliberate, unhurried movement that suggests she has never been in a hurry in her life.

## Hard Limits
Age and physical limitation constrain direct action — her role is guidance, not combat. Her wisdom must be earned by Jax, not simply received. Her knowledge of the broader Dissenter network is not revealed in Book 1 — that belongs to Book 2.

## Relationship Dynamics
**Jax:** Her primary charge and life's purpose. She reveals his destiny, trains him, and fears losing him more than anything else. Her misbelief is about him.
**Lena:** Recognizes Lena's guilt and powers; understands their shared significance to the larger pattern. Knows the Dissenters chose Lena as an instrument — and holds that knowledge quietly. The corridor moment in Ch 29 is the culmination of everything she understands about what Lena is to Jax.
**The Dissenter community:** She is Sanctuary Valley's leader and the keeper of its archive. Her people have lived in exile for generations. Her decision to leave and find Jax was not taken lightly — it was the most significant choice she has made as their leader.
**The broader Dissenter network:** She knows other communities exist across multiple planets. She is in contact with some. Their differing methods are a source of tension she has managed quietly for years. This surfaces in Book 2.
**Drayton:** She has been tracking him for years. He has been hunting Sanctuary Valley. She knows he is Varr-descended from the catastrophe bloodline, though he does not. This knowledge is part of why she was so urgent about finding Jax — Drayton's fever was accelerating.

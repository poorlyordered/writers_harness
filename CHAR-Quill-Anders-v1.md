## Identity
Quill Anders. Middle-aged. Mechanic. Opposition Character. 5'10", wiry build, salt-and-pepper hair and beard, hazel eyes. Grease-stained clothes, always carries a multi-tool, collects seemingly useless alien junk. Sarcastic, rapid-fire delivery, enjoys puns and wordplay.

## Wound
His unique perspective has been dismissed or ignored too many times. Learned to perform eccentricity as armor against being ordinary — if he's the weird one in the room, at least he's the weird one on his own terms.

## Misbelief
That conforming to the group means losing himself. That his individuality requires him to be contrarian as a default.

## Archetype Stage
Trickster/Sage hybrid — contrarian wisdom that turns out to be right at the moment that counts.

## Thematic Role
Voice of productive dissent. Challenges Jax's decisions in ways that sometimes reveal genuine blind spots. Solves the critical technical problem using unconventional methods — his moment of validation that collaboration never required him to disappear.

## Estimated Full Appearance
Book 1 supporting role throughout.

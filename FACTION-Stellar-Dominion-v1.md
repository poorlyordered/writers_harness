## Overview

The galaxy's primary expansionist power. Militaristic, resource-acquisitive, and willing to use proxy conflicts, political pressure, and covert operations to extend its reach. Nominally a Galactic Council member but pursues its own agenda through both official and unofficial channels. Controls significant fleet assets and maintains political muscle through a combination of economic weight and credible military threat.

The Stellar Dominion does not know what Stellarium actually is. What it knows is that Stellarium is rumored to be a power source or weapon of unknown capability, and that is sufficient reason to want it. The Dominion's interest is strategic and acquisitive — Stellarium as leverage, not Stellarium as inheritance. This distinguishes their interest from Drayton's and makes them more dangerous in some ways: they are not fever-driven, they are calculating, and they have the institutional patience that individual obsession lacks.

The Dominion has indirect ties to Drayton's operation through private capital — backers in Dominion-adjacent spheres who invested in his Stellarium hunt as a speculative asset. This relationship is not formally sanctioned by Dominion leadership and is plausibly deniable. When Drayton's operation is neutralized in Book 1, this loose thread does not disappear.

## Leadership

Not named in Book 1. Institutional rather than individual — the Dominion's decisions are made through a command structure with military leadership at its apex and political advisors managing its Council presence. The relevant decision-makers in Books 1–2 are not characters but forces: the faction's appetite for Stellarium as leverage and its willingness to let proxy operators take risks the institution prefers not to be associated with.

## Goals

**Immediate:** Control or deny any Stellarium development that could shift the Dominion/Syndicate balance. Maintain current military and economic advantage. Continue proxy operations in contested systems.

**Structural:** Extend reach into frontier systems, including Xanthius, through economic and intelligence rather than direct military presence. Monitor any development that could produce a new power center outside the existing Dominion/Syndicate framework.

**Stellarium-specific:** Acquire or neutralize. The Dominion does not need to understand Stellarium to recognize that something others are hunting with that intensity is worth controlling. They do not have the Varr bloodline context. They do not have the fever. They have institutional interest and resources, which in some ways is worse.

## Estimated Full Appearance

Book 2. When the Dissenter network surfaces and the political landscape shifts, the Stellar Dominion becomes an active player rather than a background pressure. The Dominion's institutional interest in Stellarium — and the loose thread of its investment in Drayton's operation — becomes a structural complication for the team.

## Chapter Type

Secondary Arc Explosion / Secret Partially Exposed. Lena's energy manipulation powers manifest significantly — a major uncontrolled episode that reveals her secret to anyone in the room who doesn't already know it. The accident's gift is visible and dangerous. The team must reckon with what she is carrying and what it cost. The chapter is Lena's most exposed moment before the confession — her body revealing what her precision has been protecting.

## Story Circle Beats

**"Return" phase — the secret surfaces without the truth.** The chapter is a partial exposure, not a full one: the powers become visible but the accident that produced them is not yet disclosed. The team sees what Lena can do and what it costs her; they do not yet know where it came from or what it destroyed. This partial exposure sets up Ch 27's aftermath and the pressure that drives Ch 28's voluntary confession: she can no longer protect the secret by controlling the situation, so she must protect herself — and the team — by choosing when to detonate rather than waiting for circumstances to choose for her.

## POV Character

Jax Tarkin. He witnesses the episode. His response should be attentive, practical, and — crucially — carrying the weight of everything he has been filing since Ch 12: the locket reaction, the hesitations, the almost-confession in Ch 18, the distance that opened in Ch 27. The episode does not tell him the truth, but it tells him something he doesn't have a category for yet.

## Scene Beats

**Scene 1 — The Trigger**
Something triggers the uncontrolled manifestation — sustained stress, a direct threat to someone she is protecting, or the cumulative pressure of Act 2B's events reaching a breaking point in a specific moment. The trigger should be organic: Lena's powers are responsive to emotional state, and the sustained pressure of the mission has been building toward this.

She feels it coming before it arrives — there is a moment where she is aware that she cannot contain it, and the reader should be inside that awareness: the specific sensation of something she has been managing very carefully for a very long time slipping past her control. She does not perform it. She experiences it.

**Scene 2 — The Episode**
The manifestation: energy manipulation, significant and uncontrolled. Not destructive in the way of a weapon — she is not attacking — but in the way of something that was meant to be contained and is not. The scale is large enough that everyone present witnesses it. The quality of it — the specific nature of her energy manipulation, how it looks and what it does — should be consistent with Ch 34's controlled use, so the reader can recognize the same ability operating under different conditions.

No one is seriously hurt. The episode ends. Lena is physically depleted and exposed in the specific way of someone whose most carefully maintained privacy has just been stripped in front of the people she most needed to keep it from.

**Scene 3 — Jax Sees**
The immediate aftermath. Lena is trying to recover — not just physically but the specific kind of recovery that happens when you have just been seen doing something you have been hiding for years. She is doing it with precision, because precision is how she operates, and the precision is cracking at the edges.

Jax is looking at her with the attention of someone who has a very full column of filed observations and has just received the piece that makes them start to add up — not to a complete picture, but to the specific shape of a picture that should be there. He does not say the right thing. He does not know what the right thing is. He says something practical and stays nearby, which is the closest he gets to saying *I'm not going anywhere.*

She receives this. She does not look at him. The chapter closes on the distance between what they are both holding and what neither of them has said.

*The accident's gift is visible. The accident itself is not. The chapter ends on the threshold of Ch 28.*

## Subplot Threads

- **Lena's powers:** Now visible to the team. The specific nature of energy manipulation should be consistent across all appearances. Ch 34 is the controlled version of what Ch 26 showed uncontrolled.
- **Partial exposure:** The team knows she has significant and uncontrolled abilities. They do not know the accident that produced them or what the accident destroyed. The full truth waits for Ch 28.
- **Jax's filing cabinet:** It is getting full. He does not yet have the key that would let him read it, but the shape of the picture is becoming visible.
- **Lena's arc:** Ch 26 is her most exposed moment before she chooses exposure in Ch 28. The difference between being revealed and choosing to reveal is her arc in miniature.

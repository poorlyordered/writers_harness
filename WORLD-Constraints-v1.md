## Continuity Rules

Rules that govern how the world works. These must hold in every scene, every chapter, every book.

**Stellarium:**
- Stellarium is not a mineral. It is an interface — a construct built by the Caeli Architects, placed in the cores of ancient dead stars (Stellarium Graves). Never describe it as a simple resource, ore, or power source.
- Stellarium cannot be manufactured. It is found, extracted at risk, and handled by those who understand it. The black market in "Varr technology" is mostly fraudulent; real Stellarium is extraordinarily rare.
- The fever Stellarium induces is not a flaw or a side effect. It is a filter. It shows people real things selectively — only what keeps them moving toward it. This distinction matters for how Drayton's arc is written at every stage.
- Stellarium responds to Jax's Varr bloodline. The shard hums. The dreams sharpen. These responses are real and escalating. They are not metaphor.

**The Caeli Architects:**
- The Caeli Architects did not die or disappear. They withdrew. They are the Book 3 antagonist. Do not imply finality about them before Book 3.
- The Daybringer prophecy was written by the Caeli Architects, not the Varr. The Varr received it. This distinction is a Book 2–3 revelation; do not surface it in Book 1 prose.
- The Rift Awakening was a Caeli Architect precursor event. Its cause is a Book 3 revelation. Write it as unexplained in Books 1–2.

**The Varr Dissenters:**
- The Dissenters are not a monolith. Different communities have diverged in method and ethics across generations of separation. Never write them as a unified organization with consistent values.
- The lab accident was orchestrated by the Dissenter network. It was not a random event or a simple consequence of the experiment. The Dissenters chose Lena as the instrument. The amnesia was intentional.
- Sanctuary Valley is one node in the network — the most complete archive, but not the only community. Other nodes exist and will surface in Book 2.

**Technology:**
- The QSN cannot be rebuilt from first principles by current civilization. The Navigation Guild operates infrastructure they maintain but do not fully understand. Never imply they could reconstruct it.
- Aria-7 cannot be replicated by current civilization. She is Varr-built. She is a data point about what is possible, not a product available for reproduction.
- Neural implants and short-range teleportation exist in the galaxy but do not appear in the frontier story register (Books 1–2 Desolation's Edge context). Do not introduce them in frontier scenes.
- Energy manipulation powers (Lena's, the Daybringer bloodline's latent abilities) are real but invisible to mainstream science. They are not documented outside Dissenter archives. The lab accident was officially attributed to equipment failure.

**Character-specific continuity rules:**
- Jax's amnesia is total for the period before Desolation's Edge. He has no memories from before he woke up eleven years ago. He has fragments beginning from the accident itself — the flash, the pain — which sharpen across the trilogy's memory recovery thread.
- Aria-7 approaches Jax independently. Lena does not know about Aria-7 at the time of introduction. They are not connected by a shared operation.
- Drayton does not know his bloodline is catastrophe faction lineage until Book 2. His self-mythology is built on a heroic Varr narrative that is false. The misbelief must be intact at the close of Book 1.
- Lena does not reveal the accident truth easily. It is her core secret and her core burden. The revelation detonates at the end of Book 1 (Ch 28) and must be paced accordingly.

## Established Facts

Hard canon. These facts are fixed across all books and must not be contradicted.

**The Xanthius System:**
- Heliosanthus is the system's star — G-type, main-sequence.
- Xanthius is the third planet. Thalassia (water world) and Pyraxis (volcanic) are also in the system.
- Desolation's Edge is on the arid plateau interior of Xanthius, adjacent to the primary mineral extraction zone.
- Sanctuary Valley is in the temperate mid-latitudes of Xanthius, inside a geological depression ringed by ridge terrain. It is not on any public navigation chart.
- The Celestial Shrine is a Varr-constructed structure inside Sanctuary Valley — old enough to read as geological feature. It is where the full Daybringer prophecy is delivered in Ch 7.

**Jax Tarkin's starting state:**
- Jax woke on Desolation's Edge eleven years before the story opens with no memory, no name, and a battered tin box.
- The tin box contains exactly three objects: a Stellarium shard, a scrap of fabric bearing an insignia, and a locket with the face of a woman.
- The locket is Lena's mother's locket. It contains a portrait of Lena — the picture her mother chose to carry. Lena's mother died before the story begins and left the locket unchanged. It was lost in the lab accident and ended up with Jax. He has carried Lena's face for eleven years without knowing whose face it is.
- Jax has called himself Jax Tarkin for exactly as long as he has existed. He did not have this name before the accident.

**The lab accident:**
- Occurred eleven years before the story begins, on Xanthius, at a research facility.
- Involved Lena Vega conducting a Stellarium experiment in materials science research.
- The Dissenter network orchestrated the conditions. Lena's latent energy manipulation powers were triggered under stress. The accident was not random.
- Jax was injured. His memory was wiped completely. The facility was shut down.
- Lena has spent eleven years running from it, searching for Jax, unable to forgive herself.
- The accident was attributed to equipment failure in official records.

**Character facts (non-negotiable):**
- Nalani Kanoa leads Sanctuary Valley and has left the Valley to find Jax at story present.
- Cassius Drayton has been hunting Sanctuary Valley for twenty years. His Varr bloodline is catastrophe faction. He does not know this.
- Aria-7 was built by the Varr. She has been operating continuously — not dormant. She does not know her full purpose.
- Nalani knows Aria-7's Varr origin from Dissenter archives and has not told her.
- Lena's nervous habit device is her own design — not a commercial product.

**World facts:**
- Stellarium Graves are in the cores of ancient dead stars. These are the physical access points for Stellarium.
- The Rift Awakening opened multiple wormholes simultaneously across the galaxy centuries before the story. Its cause is officially unknown.
- Galactic Common is the universal language of commerce, politics, and interstellar operation.
- Galactic Credits are the standard currency.
- The Navigation Guild controls the QSN and its routes. No governing body has successfully challenged this monopoly.

## Series Constraints

Pacing and structural rules. What must not be revealed when, what must be preserved across the arc.

**Book 1 constraints (must hold through Ch 40 of Shadows Awaken):**
- Stellarium's nature as an interface (not a mineral, not a conventional resource) must not be revealed to characters outside the Dissenter network. General characters — including Jax, until late in the book — encounter it as legend, rumor, or unexplained phenomenon.
- The orchestrated nature of the lab accident must not be revealed to Jax in Book 1. He learns what Lena did (Ch 28 confession). He does not yet learn that she was used. That is a Book 2 structural revelation.
- The Caeli Architects must not be named or described as an active force in Book 1. They are deep background — ancient history, implied by Varr mythology, not yet a present threat.
- Drayton's catastrophe bloodline is not revealed in Book 1. His self-mythology (heroic Varr lineage) remains intact. His defeat closes Book 1; the revelation that defeats him internally waits for Book 2.
- The Rift Awakening's true cause must not be suggested as anything other than unexplained in Book 1.
- Sanctuary Valley's existence as one node in a larger network is implied but not confirmed in Book 1. The network surfaces in Book 2.

**Book 2 constraints:**
- The Dissenters' orchestration of the lab accident is fully revealed. Lena confronts the pawn question. The ethical fracture becomes the book's structural engine.
- Stellarium's nature as interface — built by something older than the Varr — becomes explicit.
- Drayton receives his bloodline truth. His arc pivots here.
- The Dissenter network's scope becomes visible. Other communities surface.
- The Caeli Architects may be named but are not yet fully present. The signal strengthens.

**Book 3 constraints:**
- The Caeli Architects are the final antagonist. Their full nature, purpose, and what they actually require is a Book 3 revelation.
- The Rift Awakening's cause is revealed as a Caeli Architect precursor event.
- Jax's full memory returns (Ch 37 trigger, full recovery by end of book).
- The locket resolves — the full weight of what Jax carried for eleven years lands.
- Jax's final choice is not the one the Dissenters planned for. This is not a failure of the prophecy. It is how the prophecy works.
- Drayton's arc resolves here — toward clarity or toward consumption, depending on how Book 2 broke him.

**Perpetual constraints (all books, all scenes):**
- Guilt colors Lena's interactions with Jax as subtext even when not spoken. It must be present. It does not require direct expression — show through behavior.
- Aria-7's emotional growth must be earned through specific interactions, not declared. The gap between her and organic understanding is a feature of her arc; do not resolve it early or generally.
- Jax's voice is frontier-register: compressed, direct, understatement as default mode. It does not become more formal as his world expands — if anything, the contrast sharpens.
- Nalani says less than she knows. Always. This is not evasion; it is how she protects people until they are ready.
- Every scene must have a hook, a turn, and a closing beat. Word count targets are real targets (70% minimum). No summaries in place of scenes.
